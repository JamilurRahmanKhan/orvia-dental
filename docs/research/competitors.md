# Competitor Teardown — US General/Family Dental Websites (Austin TX market)

Research thread: competitor teardown for Orvia Dental (Austin, TX demo). 10 sites: Austin local practices, a national DSO, a design-forward chain, and dental-website vendors whose feature lists reveal what practice owners buy.

## Cross-cutting counts (of the 8 sites with a public patient-facing storefront reviewed above: Forest Family, Mi Casa, McLane, Magnolia, Austin Family DDS, Tend, Aspen Dental, Dentologie)

- **Show actual prices:** 2 of 8 clearly (Forest Family — $363 exam; Magnolia — $119 exam/$79 emergency/monthly financing figures for implants & Invisalign). The rest hide pricing behind "request appointment" or phone. [forestfamily.com](https://www.forestfamily.com/), [magnoliadentistryatx.com](https://www.magnoliadentistryatx.com/)
- **True real-time online booking (not just a request form):** 2 of 8 (Tend, Forest Family via Flexbook). Aspen Dental has a dedicated scheduler URL but flow/step count could not be confirmed directly (bot-blocked). Mi Casa, McLane, Austin Family DDS run on request-forms or phone-first. [hellotend.com](https://www.hellotend.com/), [forestfamily.com](https://www.forestfamily.com/), [aspendental.com/schedule-an-appointment](https://www.aspendental.com/schedule-an-appointment/)
- **Show an explicit insurance list or clear insurance messaging:** 3 of 8 (Mi Casa — "50+ PPO plans" + Medicaid; Forest Family — "most PPO plans"; Magnolia — "we file claims for all plans"). None publish a literal named-payer list on the homepage. [micasafamilydentistry.com](https://www.micasafamilydentistry.com/), [forestfamily.com](https://www.forestfamily.com/), [magnoliadentistryatx.com](https://www.magnoliadentistryatx.com/)
- **Emergency handling as a distinct, marketed path:** 3 of 8 (Mi Casa — dedicated emergency care service; Magnolia — "$79 same-day" CTA + dedicated emergency page; Aspen Dental — "same-day/next-day for pain" messaging). [micasafamilydentistry.com](https://www.micasafamilydentistry.com/), [magnoliadentistryatx.com](https://www.magnoliadentistryatx.com/), [aspendental.com](https://www.aspendental.com/)
- **In-house membership plan for uninsured patients:** 2 of 8 (Forest Family "FFD Membership," Magnolia "$300/year" plan). Industry-wide, in-house membership plans are a known pattern for practices addressing uninsured/underinsured patients. [forestfamily.com](https://www.forestfamily.com/), [magnoliadentistryatx.com](https://www.magnoliadentistryatx.com/), [pearly.co on in-house membership plans](https://www.pearly.co/dentistry-huddle/what-are-in-house-dental-membership-plans)
